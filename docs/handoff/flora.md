# Handoff: ground cover and plants (flora)

Status: **steps 1 to 9 done** (data, ground cover in detail and LOD,
placeholder textures, plant storage and generation, procedural meshes and
the foliage program, richer recipes, world clock and growth, spreading and
harvest). Step 10 is the plan for future work, in
[Step 10](#step-10-future-work-plans). How to make new plants as a modder:
`docs/modding/plants.md`. This document is a long-term roadmap. It is implemented in the
small, separately shippable steps listed in
[Implementation steps](#7-implementation-steps); see
[Progress](#9-progress) for what was built and where. Trees are a separate
plan (`docs/handoff/trees.md`) that reuses the plant pipeline described here.

## Why

Generated terrain is bare: soils, rocks and water only. Grass and forage are
the next step toward a living world. The style target is Ragnarok Online:
grass is *painted into the ground* rather than modelled blade by blade, while
bushes and plants are dense, painted, volumetric clumps whose colours blend
with the ground around them.

## Decisions (answers to the open questions)

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Is grass a block? | **No.** Plain ground grass is a *ground cover*: an overlay on the faces of the soil tile it grows on. |
| Are plants blocks? | **No.** Plants are *instances* in a per-chunk side store, anchored on a supporting tile. Blocks above the surface count as player edits (see below), and a block cannot express sub-tile size, position, growth or per-plant variation. |
| A plant bigger or taller than one tile? | Plants carry a `footprint_tiles` (bushes) or `radius_m` (small plants) and a height. Tall plants simply extend upward; footprints can cross chunk borders (deterministic ownership rule below). |
| Several plants on one 1 m tile? | Yes. Positions are sub-tile (1/16 tile). Small plants share tiles freely, over a ground cover. |
| Collision and building? | Small plants never block and are cleared when a block is placed over them. Large plants (bushes) block placement on their footprint and reserve a collision type (`none` / `soft` / `solid`) for when physics exists. |
| How are plants rendered? | Procedural meshes generated from detailed JSON recipes (crossed cards, tufts, clumps of painted leaf cards on an ellipsoid shell with spherical normals), drawn instanced with alpha cutout. No model files. |
| Do plants grow and die? | Yes, through *analytic growth*: a plant's stage is a function of its age, so unloaded areas need no ticking. Requires a world clock. |
| Relation to placeables (tables, beds, torches)? | Plants and placeables share the core: anchor tile, footprint, occupancy index and per-chunk persistence. Placeables add glTF meshes and, for stateful objects, EnTT entities. The glTF loader arrives with placeables, not with flora. |
| Grass on the underside of blocks? | Never. Undersides and cave ceilings get *hanging* flora kinds later. |
| Art? | Placeholder textures generated by a script; keys stay stable so real art can replace them file by file. |

## What exists today (and the constraints it sets)

- **Tile:** `Tile` (`src/game/world/tile.hpp`) is 4 bytes: `block_id`, liquid,
  liquid level, two 4-bit light levels. `isSolid()`/`isVisible()` are just
  `block_id != 0`.
- **Edits are "solid above the generated surface":** `ChunkManager::isEdited`
  marks a chunk edited when any tile's `isSolid() != (worldY <= surface_y)`, and
  `TerrainEditTracker` records solid tiles above the surface as *structure
  voxels* for distant terrain (`terrain_edits.dat`). Sunlight treats any
  non-air tile as the column top. Plants as blocks would mark nearly every
  chunk as edited and fill the distant edit index with grass. This is the main
  reason plants are not blocks.
- **Data types:** registered through `IAssets::registerDataType<T>`
  (`src/engine/core/assets_api.hpp`), order set in
  `src/render/resource_manager.cpp` and `assets/definitions.json`. Ids are
  `uint8_t`, assigned in load order (max 255 per type) and saved raw, so new
  types and files go **last** in the manifest.
- **Placement rules to reuse:** `SoilPlacement` and the `soil_json::range` /
  `soil_json::enums` validators (`src/game/items/soil.hpp`);
  `SoilGenerator::allowed` / `select`, the hash `SoilGenerator::unit` and the
  X-periodic noise `SoilGenerator::field` (`src/game/world/soil_generator.hpp`).
- **Climate is per region** (128×128 tiles, `src/game/world/region.hpp`):
  biome, land type, features, humidity, temperature, `VegetationType`.
  `VegetationType` (GRASS / SHRUB / FOREST / RAINFOREST) is computed in
  `world_generator.cpp` but today only decides whether topsoil exists.
  Columns get `surface_y`, slope and a `SoilColumn` from
  `TerrainSampler::columns`, with soils blended across region borders.
- **Generation hook:** `LocationGenerator::generateColumn` / `generateChunk`
  (`src/game/world/location_generator.{hpp,cpp}`), run on worker jobs from
  immutable neighbourhood captures. Output must not depend on job order.
  `isAirChunk` skips chunks above the max surface (plants need headroom).
- **Saves:** saved chunks are decoded, never regenerated
  (`save/chunk_codec.hpp`, v1 palette `{block, liquid, level}`). Region files
  accept new fourcc sections; older readers keep unknown sections. Bump
  `WorldMeta::GeneratorVersion` when generation output changes.
- **Rendering:**
  - One opaque `DrawLayer::GAME`; no alpha test or blending in the world pass.
  - Greedy mesher (`chunk_mesher.cpp`): one material layer per block, used on
    all faces. `BlockVertex` has unused `tint` and `uv` attributes.
  - Triplanar world-space texturing from a 128² RGBA8 albedo/normal array
    (`ResourceManager::buildMaterialTextureArrays`); alpha is available.
  - Per-chunk 18³ light volume (`u_light_volume`, RGBA = sun, block light,
    chroma) sampled in `shaders/block/block.frag`; `shaders/world_surface.sh`
    holds shared lighting and fog.
  - Instancing exists: `RenderTarget::drawInstanced`, used by the light glare
    in `light_renderer.cpp` (template for plants).
  - No model, mesh-asset or billboard pipeline for world objects.
  - Distant terrain (`terrain_lod`) uses a patch material map with RG = surface
    layer and BA = rock layer; the texel is full.
- **Camera:** orbit / top-down, 10–150 m from the target. Detailed voxels
  reach about 128 tiles (`DETAIL_CHUNK_RADIUS = 8`); LOD covers the rest.
- **No world clock or calendar** exists yet.

## 1. Three-layer model

| Layer | Examples | Storage | Rendering |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ground cover** | grass, moss, lichen, leaf litter, alpine turf | Per tile: `cover` id + `coverState` on the *solid* tile whose faces show it | Overlay in `block.frag`: top face, hanging side fringe, noise-dithered density edges |
| **Plants** | tall grass, flowers, ferns, herbs, forage, bushes | Per chunk: list of `FloraInstance` anchored on a supporting tile | New instanced `foliage` program, procedural meshes, alpha cutout |
| **Placeables** (separate plan) | tables, beds, torches | Shares anchor / footprint / occupancy / persistence core | glTF meshes; EnTT entities for stateful objects |

## 2. Ground cover

### Data: `GroundCover` (`assets/data/ground_covers/*.json`)

```jsonc
{
  "name": "ground_covers.meadow_grass",
  "overlay_material": "materials.cover_meadow_grass", // albedo with alpha mask + normal
  "faces": { "top": true, "sides": true, "side_fringe_m": [0.25, 0.45] },
  "soils": { "names": ["soils.loam", "soils.silt_loam"],
             "fertility": [0.3, 1.0], "water_retention": [0.2, 1.0] },
  "placement": [ { /* same fields as SoilPlacement: allowed_land, allowed_biomes,
                    required/forbidden_features, humidity_range,
                    temperature_range_c, priority, weight */ } ],
  "density_noise": { "scale_m": 24, "threshold": 0.35, "contrast": 1.5 },
  "max_slope": 0.8,
  "requires_sky": true,
  "tint_gradient": { "humid_warm": "#4f8a3a", "humid_cold": "#3d6b45",
                     "dry_warm": "#9a9a45", "dry_cold": "#7b7f52" }
}
```

`tint_gradient` gives RO-like variation (olive and yellow patches next to deep
greens) with one texture.

### Storage

`Tile` grows from 4 to 6 bytes:

- `uint8_t cover`: `GroundCover` id, 0 = none.
- `uint8_t coverState`: low nibble = density 0–15; high nibble reserved
  (trampled, withered, snow-covered).

Keeping cover on the tile means it survives edits, palette compression and
`withChunkLocked` without a new side structure. `chunk_codec` v2 adds both
bytes to each palette entry; the v1 decoder sets them to 0. Cover never
changes `isSolid`, sunlight, opacity or edit detection.

### Generation

In `LocationGenerator::generateColumn`, the topmost topsoil tile with
`surfaceY >= SEA_LEVEL_Y` gets a cover selected by rules (soil, region climate,
`VegetationType`) and a density from periodic noise
(`SoilGenerator::field` with `TerrainNeighborhood::periodX()`), lowered by
slope. Selection blends across region borders the way soils do in
`TerrainSampler::soil`. Bump `GeneratorVersion`.

### Rendering

- The greedy mesher adds the cover id to `FaceKey`, so faces with different
  covers do not merge. Density is **not** a per-vertex value.
- Density is uploaded next to the chunk light volume (a second R8 page, or
  packed into the existing slot layout; decide during step 2) and sampled
  bilinearly in the shader. It fades smoothly across tiles without breaking
  greedy quads.
- The overlay is blended over the soil albedo by
  `smoothstep(noiseMask - density)`: painted, ragged edges instead of tile
  squares. Normals blend the same way.
- Side faces get the fringe by distance below the tile's top edge
  (`fract(v_position.y)`) when the tile above is air; length from
  `side_fringe_m` with per-tile jitter.
- Bottom faces never get cover.

### Edits

`WorldEditor` clears (or marks withered) the cover of a tile when a solid
block is placed directly above it; digging a tile removes its cover. Cover
regrowth onto exposed topsoil belongs to the growth step.

### Distant terrain

Add a second RGBA8 patch map (cover layer, density, tint) sampled in
`terrain_lod.frag` with the same blend, and carry cover in `SurfaceColumn`
(`surface_sampler.hpp`). Distant hills read green; check the detail/LOD
handover for colour seams.

## 3. Plants: data

Two data types: `Flora` (species) and `FoliageMaterial`. A species is a
**recipe of parts**; each instance's seed samples every range, so no two
plants are identical. **Every numeric field accepts a number or a
`[min, max]` range.**

### `Flora` (`assets/data/flora/*.json`)

```jsonc
{
  "name": "flora.round_bush",
  "kind": "bush",              // grass_tuft | flower | fern | herb | bush | shrub | succulent (hanging later)
  "footprint": { "tiles": [2, 2], "blocks_placement": true, "collision": "soft",
                 "min_spacing_m": 1.5 },   // small plants: "radius_m": 0.2
  "placement": {
    "soils": { "fertility": [0.3, 1], "water_retention": [0.25, 1] },
    "ground_cover": { "required": ["ground_covers.meadow_grass"], "forbidden": [] },
    "climate": [ { /* SoilPlacement fields */ } ],
    "max_slope": 0.6, "underwater": false,
    "density_per_100m2": [0.5, 2],
    "cluster": { "scale_m": 18, "threshold": 0.55 },   // patches, not uniform noise
    "companions": [ { "species": "flora.fern", "radius_m": 3, "chance": 0.4 } ],
    "priority": 10
  },
  "growth": {
    "stages": { "seedling": [3, 6], "young": [10, 20], "mature": [40, 80],
                "flowering": [8, 14], "fruiting": [10, 20],
                "withering": [6, 10] },               // game days
    "perennial": true, "lifespan_days": [400, 900],
    "season_mask": null,                              // after the calendar exists
    "spread": { "radius_m": 4, "chance_per_cycle": 0.15 },
    "regrow_after_harvest_days": [10, 20]
  },
  "parts": [
    { "type": "leaf_clump", "count": [3, 5],
      "shell": { "radii_m": [[0.7, 1.0], [0.5, 0.8], [0.7, 1.0]], "lift_m": [0.3, 0.5] },
      "cards_per_clump": [18, 30], "card_size_m": [0.35, 0.55],
      "normal_mode": "spherical",
      "material": "foliage.broadleaf", "color_variation": 0.12,
      "stages": { "seedling": 0.2, "young": 0.6, "mature": 1.0, "withering": 0.9 } },
    { "type": "fruit_cluster", "count": [2, 6], "material": "foliage.red_berry",
      "attach": "leaf_clump_surface", "stages": { "fruiting": 1.0 } }
  ],
  "forage": [ { "item": "items.red_berry", "count": [2, 6], "stage": "fruiting" } ]
}
```

Part `type`s: `stem`, `blade`, `leaf_card`, `leaf_clump`, `petal_ring`,
`flower_head`, `fruit_cluster`, `frond`. Shared part parameters: `count`,
`height_m`, `length_m`, `width_m`, `bend`, `droop`, `spread_angle`,
`phyllotaxis`, `shell`, `normal_mode` (`spherical` | `card` | `cylindrical`),
`material`, `color_variation`, `attach`, and `stages` (the growth stages in
which the part appears, with a scale per stage).

`forage` refers to items that do not exist yet; the loader stores names and
validates them only once an items type exists.

### `FoliageMaterial` (`assets/data/foliage_materials/*.json`)

```jsonc
{
  "name": "foliage.broadleaf",
  "albedo": "foliage.leaf_broad",      // greyscale-ish detail with alpha cutout
  "normal": "foliage.leaf_broad_normal",
  "palette": { "base": "#4c7d33", "shade": "#2c4a26", "tip": "#9bbf4a", "dry": "#8a7a3a" },
  "gradient": "root_to_tip",
  "hue_jitter": 0.04, "value_jitter": 0.12,
  "translucency": 0.35,
  "withered": ["#8a7a3a", "#5e4a2a"]
}
```

Textures carry shape and detail; colour comes from the palette, gradient and
per-instance jitter. One leaf texture serves many species without looking
flat, and the palette can be pulled toward the local ground-cover tint so
plants and grass share a hue family.

## 4. Plants: instances, storage, simulation

### `FloraInstance` (about 12 bytes)

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `species` | u8 | `Flora` id |
| `seed` | u16 | drives every per-instance range and shader variation |
| `x`, `z` | u8 each | chunk-local position in 1/16 tile |
| `y` | u8 | chunk-local anchor tile (the supporting tile) |
| `rotation`, `scale` | u8 each | quantized yaw and scale factor |
| `birthDay` | i32 | game day of germination (may be negative for generated plants) |
| `flags` | u8 | harvested, damaged, reserved |

### Analytic growth

A plant's stage and stage progress are a pure function of
`(now - birthDay, species, seed, environment)`. Unloaded areas need no ticking:
a plant is evaluated at the current day when its chunk loads. Death, removal
and offspring (from `spread`) are resolved lazily on chunk load and by a slow
background sweep over loaded chunks. This follows the vision's rule that
simulation detail can vary with distance. Generated plants get `birthDay`
values spread into the past so a new world starts with mixed ages.

### Storage and persistence

- `FloraLayer`: a per-chunk side store owned by `ChunkManager` (like the light
  chroma side buffer), produced by the chunk job next to the tiles.
- Saved as a new region-file section `FLOR` (one blob per chunk, same local
  index as `CHNK`) whenever the chunk is persisted. Unedited chunks that are
  regenerated produce the same plants (deterministic generation).

### Deterministic placement across chunk borders

An instance belongs to the chunk of its anchor tile. Generation evaluates
candidates for the 3×3 chunk-column neighbourhood (a jittered grid per species,
seeded by **world** tile coordinates and X-periodic), keeps only candidates
anchored in the current chunk, and resolves conflicts (spacing, footprints) by
a fixed rule: higher `priority` first, then lower hash. Neighbours reach the
same answer without communicating, and job order does not matter.

### Occupancy index

A per-chunk set of tiles blocked by large-plant footprints, rebuilt from
`FloraLayer`. This is the core that placeables reuse.

### Edits

- `WorldEditor` removes plants whose anchor tile is dug, and small plants
  whose volume a new block fills.
- Placement over a blocking footprint is refused.
- Raycast against plant bounds comes with interaction (harvest).

### Prerequisite: world clock

A minimal `WorldClock` (game day and time of day, saved in `world.json`, with
a debug speed control). It is shared groundwork with day/night and with
[light-query.md](light-query.md).

## 5. Plants: meshes and rendering

- **`FloraMeshBuilder`** (CPU, worker-safe) builds a mesh from
  `(species, variant seed, stage)`. A cache keeps *N variants × stages* per
  species (for example 8 × 4), built on workers on demand.
- **Per-instance uniqueness in the shader** on top of the shared variant:
  rotation, non-uniform scale, hue and value jitter from the seed, wind phase
  and a withering blend. Meshes stay shared and instanced; every plant still
  looks different.
- **Vertex format:** position, normal (spherical for clumps), uv, foliage
  material layer, and a part attribute (root-to-tip height for the colour
  gradient and wind weight).
- **`foliage` program** (`shaders/foliage/`): alpha cutout (`discard`), wind
  sway in the vertex shader, translucency/backlight term, lighting from the
  chunk light volume at the instance anchor plus far lights
  (`LightRenderer::appendUniforms`), fog from `world_surface.sh`. Drawn in
  `DrawLayer::GAME` after terrain.
- **Foliage texture array:** separate from the terrain material array (alpha
  cards, possibly 256²).
- **Instancing:** one `drawInstanced` per mesh variant per group of chunks,
  following the light-glare template. Instance data holds position,
  rotation/scale, seed and stage blend.
- **Distance:** dithered fade-out at about 80–100 m, inside the detail radius.
  Beyond it the ground-cover LOD tint stands in. Bush impostors in distant
  terrain are a later step shared with trees.
- **RO-style blending:**
  - Clump normals point away from the clump centre, so bushes shade as soft
    volumes instead of showing card edges.
  - The base of a plant darkens toward the ground colour.
  - An optional painted contact-shadow decal sits under bushes.
  - Palettes lean toward the local ground-cover tint.
- **Shadows:** plants receive far-light shadows but do not cast them at first.

## 7. Implementation steps

Each step builds, runs and is testable on its own.

1. **Data types.** `GroundCover`, `Flora`, `FoliageMaterial` structs and JSON
   parsing with validation (reuse `soil_json` helpers; number-or-range values),
   manifest registration, two or three sample definitions. Unit tests for
   parsing, range sampling and rule matching under `tests/unit/game/items/`.
2. **Ground cover v1.** `Tile` cover fields and `chunk_codec` v2; generation
   pass and `GeneratorVersion` bump; mesher key and density volume; shader
   overlay (top first, then side fringe); `WorldEditor` clearing. Tests: codec
   round trip, v1 compatibility, deterministic cover per seed, cover never
   changes `isSolid` or edit detection.
3. **Ground cover in distant terrain.** Cover patch map and the
   `terrain_lod.frag` blend; check the detail/LOD handover seam.
4. **Placeholder texture generator.**
5. **Flora storage and generation.** `FloraInstance`, `FloraLayer`,
   deterministic 3×3 candidate placement, `FLOR` section, occupancy index,
   `WorldEditor` removal and placement blocking. Tests: determinism across job
   order and chunk borders, X-wrap seam, save round trip.
6. **Procedural meshes and the `foliage` program.** `FloraMeshBuilder`
   (crossed cards and tufts first, then clumps with spherical normals),
   instanced draw, light volume sampling, cutout, distance fade. Profile with
   Tracy against a budget (target: under 2 ms for flora at the default radius
   on low-end hardware).
7. **Richer recipes.** Full part system (stems, flower heads, fruit), palettes
   and gradients, wind, translucency, per-instance shader variation, tint
   matching with ground cover.
8. **World clock and analytic growth.** Stages over time, withering and death,
   lazy evaluation on load, slow sweep for loaded chunks, cover regrowth on
   exposed topsoil.
9. **Spread and reproduction.** Offspring, death replacement, harvest flags
   and forage hooks for the future items system.
10. **Later, with other plans.** Placeables on the shared anchor / footprint /
    occupancy / section core; trees and distant impostors; seasons; hanging
    and underside flora for caves and the underworld (never assume Y 0–1024).

## 8. Open questions

- Growth speed: game-day length relative to real time; seasons or not.
- Does traffic (walking) lower cover density, so RO-style dirt paths emerge?
- Do soft collisions (bushes) slow movement once physics exists?
- Does flora feed fauna and food chains in background simulation (biomass per
  region)?
- Underwater cover: seagrass, algae?
- Plant count limits per chunk and density quality presets.

- Registered after `Soil`. Data: `assets/data/ground_covers/`,
  `foliage_materials/`, `flora/`; overlay materials in
  `materials/vegetation.json`. `generate_assets.py` lists data types in
  directory order; load order between types does not matter (ids are per

- Ground covers also accept an optional `vegetation` list and

- Tests: `tests/unit/game/items/flora_data_tests.cpp`.

### Step 2: ground cover v1 (done)

- `Tile` is 6 bytes: `cover`, `coverState` (low nibble density).
  `chunk_codec` v2 stores both in each palette entry; v1 decodes with no
  cover.
- Generation: `GroundCoverGenerator` (`world/ground_cover_generator.hpp`)
  picks one cover per region in `TerrainNeighborhood::capture` (soil,
  vegetation, climate rules; priority then weight). `TerrainSampler::soil`
  gives a column the cover of the region its soil came from (so covers blend
  across borders with soils) and a density from X-periodic noise lowered by
  slope; `LocationGenerator` puts it on the surface topsoil tile.
  `GeneratorVersion` is 6.
- Meshing: `FaceKey` includes the cover. Covered faces carry the overlay in
  `BlockVertex` attributes the shaders never used: `u` = overlay layer (-1
  none), `v` = fringe range (`ChunkMesher::packFringe`), `tint` = climate
  tint (corner climate from `ChunkManager::submitMesh`, bilinear between
  region centres). LOD joins/skirts now write `u = -1`.
- Density: `LightVolume::encodeCover`/`buildCover` and a second R8 3D page
  per `LightVolumeManager` page (`u_cover_volume`, stage 5). A cell without
  cover takes the density of a covered tile directly above or below it, so
  filtering across one-tile steps does not fade grass. Joins and structures
  bind a zero 1x1x1 texture (`TerrainLodManager`).
- Shader (`block.frag`, `applyCover`; shared math in `world_surface.sh`,
  `surfaceCover*`): top faces show a texel once `density > 1 - overlay
  alpha`; side faces get a fringe whose length is hashed per tile in
  `side_fringe_m` (up to the whole side) and scaled by alpha and density;
  broad value noise on the tint (by XZ only). Placeholder overlays come from `tools/generate_foliage_textures.py`
  (alpha is rank-equalized so coverage grows linearly with density).
- Edits: `WorldEditor` clears the cover of a tile whose block changes and of
  the tile below a placed block (also across chunk borders). Cover-only
  changes are saved and remeshed but not emitted through `blockChanged`.
- Tests: `tests/unit/game/world/ground_cover_tests.cpp` (codec versions,
  surface-only deterministic generation, blocks unchanged by cover, X seam,
  mesh faces, volume dilation, edits).
- Visual check: `build/tests/lod_render_smoke <prefix> 900 lights` (only that
  scenario binds light volumes, so only it shows cover on detailed chunks in
  the harness).
- Tuning after review: the sample covers are dense (meadow: threshold 0,
  contrast 3.5, so density saturates over most of the noise range), slopes
  thin them less (`max_slope` 1.4 to 1.8), and fringes cover most or all of
  a side face (meadow `[0.9, 1.0]`), avoiding a blocky look on terraces.

### Step 3: ground cover in distant terrain (done)

- `TerrainLod::Mesh::covers`: a second RGBA8 map per patch (R overlay layer
  + 1, G density, BA tint RGB565), uploaded as `u_cover_map` (stage 6,
  `TerrainLodManager::CoverStage`, point sampled) with `u_lod_cover.x` =
  texels per side. Stepped patches get one texel per tile (the same values
  as the voxels, so the hand-over matches); smooth patches use the material
  map's grid.
- `SurfaceSampler::cover` gives the column's generated cover, dropped where
  an edit moved the ground. Climate for the tint comes from
  `ground_cover_climate::at`, shared with `ChunkManager`.
- `terrain_lod.frag`: layer from the nearest texel, density and tint
  bilinear over neighbouring texels of the same cover; tops with `normal.y >
  0.65` (where the surface material shows). Stepped wall vertices store the
  column top in `u`, so walls get the fringe (a fixed 0.9 m length: the map
  has no room for each cover's range).
- Tests: `cover_lod_matches_detail` (stepped texels equal the detailed
  columns, wall tops, smooth grid size, edits), `cover_tint_packing`.

### Step 4: placeholder textures (done)

- `tools/generate_foliage_textures.py` also writes 256x256 plant cards to
  `assets/materials/foliage/` (keys `foliage.<card>`): `leaf_broad` (leaf
  cluster for bush clumps), `blade` (grass tuft), `frond`, `petal`, `berry`,
  each with a `_normal`. RGB is light grey detail (palettes colour it), A is
  the cutout, and cards are authored root down (bottom centre = attachment).
  They load as CPU material textures but stay out of the 128x128 terrain
  array, which only takes `Material` textures.
- `validateFoliageTextures` (called by `ResourceManager`) requires every
  foliage material texture key to be loaded.
- Data: `foliage.meadow_petal` and a `flora.meadow_flower` species (stem,
  leaves on the stem, petal ring at the tip).

### Step 5: plant storage and generation (done)

- `world/flora.hpp`: `FloraInstance` (12 bytes: species, flags, seed, x/z in
  1/16 tile, anchor y, rotation, birthDay) and `FloraLayer`; analytic stages
  in `flora_growth::at` (annuals pass each stage once; perennials grow up,
  then cycle mature/flowering/fruiting and wither at the end of their
  lifespan).
- `world/flora_generator.{hpp,cpp}`: `FloraSpeciesTable` resolves species
  rules from data (cell size from density, cover/soil bitsets, climate,
  companions, footprint, height from the recipe). `generateFlora` places
  plants for one chunk:
  - a jittered candidate grid per species, hashed by X-periodic world cell
    (`SoilGenerator::PeriodicAxis`), kept by density and cluster noise;
  - environment per tile from the chunk's `ChunkColumnInputs` (which now
    carry `slope`) or, outside it, the same `TerrainSampler` calls;
  - large plants (tile footprints) survive when no conflicting valid
    candidate outranks them (priority, then hash). This local rule needs
    only candidates within `reach()` tiles, so every chunk agrees without
    the global greedy order; footprints also need ground within one tile;
  - small plants share tiles freely and are cleared from large footprints;
    small companions spawn around kept large plants;
  - saved chunks without stored plants regrow them where the anchor is
    still solid with air above.
- `ChunkManager`: plants are made by the chunk job (only for slabs holding a
  surface tile), kept per handle (`flora()`, `setFlora()`, signal
  `floraChanged`), saved in snapshots. Region files gain section `FLOR`
  (`save/flora_codec.hpp`); `LocationManager` passes saved plants back.
  `GeneratorVersion` is 7.
- Edits (`WorldEditor`): `Blocked` when placing inside a footprint that
  blocks placement; a block placed into a plant's volume removes the plant;
  removing a block removes the plants it supported. `FloraVolume` gives the
  occupied tiles (footprint up to the species height), computed from the
  layers of neighbouring chunks rather than a separate index.
- Tests: `tests/unit/game/world/flora_tests.cpp` (codec and FLOR round trip,
  growth stages, no overlapping footprints across chunk borders and the X
  seam, identical results from either side, streaming with saved, edited and
  regrown plants, edits).

### Step 6: meshes and the foliage program (done)

- `world/flora_mesh_builder.{hpp,cpp}`: CPU builder, deterministic per
  (species, variant 0-7, stage); `FoliageVertex` in `engine/render/
  vertex.hpp`. Meshes are built on the main thread on demand (48 per frame
  at most), not on workers: each takes well under a millisecond.
- `world/flora_renderer.{hpp,cpp}`: per-chunk instance caches (rebuilt on
  `becameReady`, `floraChanged`, `lightChanged`), grouped by mesh into one
  instanced draw per mesh, 64-byte instances (position relative to the eye,
  yaw, scale, seed, ground colour, withering, sky/block light, fade
  distance). Light comes from the tile the plant stands in; far lights and
  their shadows from `world_surface.sh` (block-program stages).
- `shaders/foliage/`: alpha cutout, wind (tips sway, travelling gusts),
  dithered distance fade, double-sided card lighting, translucency.
- Fade distance grows with plant height (`FloraRenderer::fadeDistance`):
  tufts and flowers fade out by about 45-55 m, bushes at 100 m, which keeps
  the numerous small plants cheap.
- Foliage texture arrays (`ResourceManager::buildFoliageTextureArrays`,
  256x256, layer = foliage material id - 1); mips keep the cutout coverage
  of the full-size card.
- Profiling: `build/tests/lod_render_smoke <prefix> 900 lights` prints
  `plants`, `plant_draws` and `plant_cpu_ms`. On the test scene: about 500
  to 900 plants, 30 to 50 draws, 0.05-0.5 ms CPU per frame. Tracy is linked
  only into `app`, so the renderer reports its own CPU time; GPU time was
  not measured.
- Visual checks: `SMOKE_CAMERA="x z yaw pitch distance above"` frames a
  spot; `SMOKE_FLORA_GALLERY=1` plants every species by growth stage in
  rows around it.

### Step 7: richer recipes (done)

- Part types: `stem` (untextured, cylindrical normals), `blade`,
  `leaf_card` (along stems by phyllotaxis or at the base), `leaf_clump`
  (cards on an ellipsoid shell with spherical normals), `petal_ring`,
  `flower_head` (an up-facing disc), `fruit_cluster` (on clump shells or
  stem tips), `frond`. Hidden stems still anchor attached parts.
- Colour: palette gradients baked per vertex (the base colour dominates,
  shade deep or low, tip at the top), per-card `color_variation`, per-plant
  hue and value jitter in the shader, withering towards the dry colour,
  a lean of the plant's hue toward the ground cover tint and root darkening
  toward the ground colour.
- Data: materials `foliage.fern` (frond card), `foliage.small_leaf`,
  `foliage.flower_center`; the meadow flower has a stem, leaves, petals and
  a centre.

- Bushes sit on the ground: clump shells reach down to it (`lift_m` near 0
  in the data, centres at 0.45-0.6 of the vertical radius), woody `stem`
  parts show as twigs (`foliage.bark`), and each clump lays a skirt of
  near-flat leaves in the shade colour on the ground (a painted contact
  shadow).
- Bushes never stand in blocks: generation and seeding require level ground
  under the whole footprint and no ground rising in the ring beside it, and
  `FloraMeshBuilder` limits each clump's horizontal reach to the footprint
  plus 0.3 m at the largest instance scale. Live tiles (saved or re-sampled
  chunks) also need air up to the plant's height over the footprint.

### Step 8: world clock and growth (done)

- `world/world_clock.hpp`: `WorldClock`, fractional game days since the
  world began (72 real minutes per day at speed 1, the author's choice: 5% of
  a real day; see IDEA.md, "World time"), saved as `worldDays` in
  `world.json` (`WorldMeta`). The TIME button in gameplay cycles debug
  speeds (x1, x60, x720, x7200). Day/night and light queries will read
  `timeOfDay()`.
- Stages stay analytic (`flora_growth::at`); a negative age is a seed that
  has not germinated (not drawn). `FloraRenderer` evaluates stages at the
  clock's day and refreshes its chunk caches once per game day.
- `world/flora_simulation.{hpp,cpp}`, `advanceFlora`: plants past their
  death age are removed; nothing regrows in place (populations continue
  through seeds, step 9). A chunk is advanced when it loads and by a slow
  sweep over loaded chunks (`FloraSimulation::update`, a few chunks per
  frame).
- `FloraLayer::resolvedDay` (saved; `flora_codec` v2 with v1 still read)
  remembers the day a chunk was last advanced. A layer whose only change is
  this stamp is not marked for saving.
- Cover regrowth: chunks touched by edits (and saved edited chunks when
  loaded) are watched; exposed topsoil with open sky (sun exposure 15) gets
  the column's generated cover back after a day, thickening by two density
  levels per day to the generated density. It runs while loaded only.

### Step 9: spreading, death replacement and harvest (done)

- Seeding (revised after review: no rebirth in place). Per seeding cycle
  (`flora_growth::cycle`) passed since `resolvedDay` (at most 16 per resolve),
  at the end of the seed stage (`flora_growth::seedDrop`, `spread.stage`), a
  plant drops `spread.seeds` seeds within `radius_m` with probability
  `chance_per_cycle`. A seed germinates 1-10 days later when the ground
  suits the species (`floraSuits` on live tiles: soil from the block,
  cover, slope from the generated columns, climate from the region, open
  sky, no large footprint; level ground and headroom for large plants) and
  its roll is below `germination * (1 - local / target)`:
  - `local`: plants and dormant seeds of the species within the crowding
    radius (at least 2.5 m, wide enough for three plants at full density,
    at least a candidate cell for large plants);
  - `target`: the species' maximum density times `floraClusterMask` (the
    generated patches, from the unsalted flora seed) over that area.
  This logistic rule refills gaps and stops at the generated density; failed
  seeds are never stored. Seeds are deterministic (parent seed, cycle and
  index) and deduplicated, so resolving again never plants twice.
  `flora_growth::replacementRatio` estimates seedlings per plant on empty
  ground; shipped species are checked to be between 1.5 and 40.
- Long absences: a chunk not resolved for `CatchUpDays` (365, one epoch) is
  re-sampled (`ChunkManager::regenerateFlora`): generation with the flora
  seed salted by the epoch (`floraEpochSalt`) and the cluster patches fixed,
  ages counted back from today, on live tiles; large plants overlapping
  those of neighbours resolved otherwise are dropped. Two per update; others
  wait. New chunks also generate for the current day's epoch
  (`ChunkManager::setFloraDay`), so a region first visited late needs no
  re-sampling.
- Measured (test meadow, 3 years in 5-day steps, inner 3x3 chunks of a 5x5
  loaded area): grass, ferns and bushes average 1.2-1.7x their generated
  count, flowers (annual, few) 0.8x; no species died out or exceeded 2.2x.
- Harvest: `FloraInstance` is 16 bytes with `harvestDay`.
  `FloraSimulation::harvest(tile, today)` picks plants standing on or
  growing into a tile, in the stage of their `forage` entries: they yield
  `ForageYield {item, count}` (names for the future items system), and are
  marked `Harvested` (regrowing after `regrow_after_harvest_days`) or
  removed when they do not regrow. Harvested plants draw their mature mesh
  (no flowers or fruit). The HARVEST tool and the tile panel expose this
  for debugging.
- Tests: `flora_world_clock`, `flora_life_cycle`,
  `flora_simulation_spread_harvest_regrow` (population stability, long
  absence, harvest, cover regrowth) in `flora_tests.cpp`.

## Step 10: future work (plans)

Not implemented; each item is a separate plan and should get its own
handoff when started.

### Placeables

Tables, beds, torches: data-defined objects placed by the player.

- Reuse the plant core: an anchor tile, a footprint (`FloraVolume`-like
  volumes and the placement refusal path in `WorldEditor`), a per-chunk side
  store saved in its own region section (`PLAC`, like `FLOR`).
- Meshes from glTF files (the loader arrives with placeables), drawn
  instanced like `FloraRenderer` for static objects.
- Stateful objects (a bed's owner, a chest's contents) get EnTT entities
  that reference their placeable; light-emitting placeables register a
  `LightSource` (`world/light_sources.hpp`) with `LightEmission` from data.
- Occupancy should become one per-chunk index shared by plants and
  placeables once lookups become frequent (today it is recomputed from
  neighbouring layers).

### Trees and distant vegetation

Trees have their own plan: `docs/handoff/trees.md` (voxel trunks, size
classes up to massive landmarks, growth, cutting). The notes below were the
first sketch; the trees handoff supersedes them where they differ.

- Trees as large flora: new part types (`trunk`, `branch`, `canopy` built on
  `leaf_clump`), `collision: solid`, footprints of 1-3 tiles, heights of 4-20
  metres (`maxHeight()` grows, so edit and render reach must follow).
- Chopping yields wood through the harvest path; a felled tree becomes a
  stump instance.
- Distant impostors: bake each tree and bush variant into a small billboard
  atlas (front, side, top) at load; LOD patches carry a per-patch instance
  list built by the LOD jobs from the same deterministic placement (only
  large species), drawn instanced with dithered cross-fade against the
  detailed plants at the fade distance. Dense forests may instead darken and
  roughen the LOD surface (a canopy layer in the cover map).
- Shadows: plants and trees cast far-light shadows (a caster pass with the
  cutout) once the shadow budget allows; trees first.

### Seasons and weather

- A calendar on the clock (days per year, configurable), seasons per
  latitude band from region temperature.
- `season_mask` on stages (flowering only in spring), autumn colours (a
  palette shift toward `dry`), deciduous trees dropping leaves (clump stage
  scale 0 in winter).
- Snow on ground cover and plants: the reserved high nibble of
  `Tile::coverState` (snow, trampled, withered) and a snow blend in the
  foliage shader.

### Hanging and underside flora

- Anchors on the underside of a tile (an instance flag), growing downward:
  vines, hanging moss, cave fungi, roots in ceilings. Covers for cave
  ceilings as "hanging" ground covers (never the plain cover on undersides).
- Caves, sky islands (around Y 2048) and the underworld (below 0): never
  assume Y in 0-1024 or that plants stand at the generated surface.

### Rare plants: discovery and quests

Rare species already work as data (very low `density_per_100m2`, strict
rules, `persistent_sites`; see `docs/modding/plants.md`, "Rare plants").
Generation scales the candidate grid to the density (one candidate per
cell, about 1000 m cells at one plant per km²; `FloraSimulation::crowd`
then visits only chunks with plants), and persistent sites are placed with
the epoch-free seed. What a quest needs next:

- **Rare-plant query** (`FloraAtlas` or similar): where a species can grow
  in a region, without loading chunks. Placement is deterministic: run the
  species' candidate grid for the region, check rules with
  `TerrainSampler` (surface, slope, soil, cover) and the region climate,
  and return the sites (persistent sites are exact; others are the current
  epoch's). Cost is small for rare species (few candidates per region).
- **Knowledge, not omniscience:** rumours, herbalists and books reveal a
  region or an area ("one of three valleys near the crater"), never the
  tile; the player still searches. Found sites go into the player's
  knowledge (map notes).
- **Harvest effects:** forage items need the items system; properties
  (healing lethal wounds) belong to the alchemy design.
- **Visibility aids** (optional): a faint glint or scent cue at short range
  for flagged species, or perception skills extending their fade distance.

### Smaller follow-ups

- Day/night cycle on `WorldClock::timeOfDay()` (sky light, then
  `docs/handoff/light-query.md`).
- Cover regrowth in unloaded areas (analytic, from an edit day stored per
  chunk) and spreading of cover onto bare topsoil next to grass.
- Walking wears paths: lower cover density where entities walk (the
  trampled nibble), regrowing over time.
- Foliage normal maps in lighting; plants casting contact shadows into the
  cover (a darkening term in `block.frag` near large plants).
- Biomass per region for background simulation (fauna food).
- Density quality presets (fade distances, tuft counts) in the settings.

Known limits: a covered tile next to an uncovered one (rock, other soil)
ends the overlay at the tile edge; cover regrows only while loaded; plants
cast no shadows; foliage normal maps are loaded but not yet used for
lighting; bushes are not drawn on distant terrain (see step 10).
