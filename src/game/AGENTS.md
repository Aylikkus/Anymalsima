# Game Guidelines

See the [repository guidelines](../../AGENTS.md) for shared conventions.

## Responsibilities & Structure

`game.hpp/.cpp` owns the active state and defers state changes through the frame lifecycle. `states/` implements menus, world creation, and gameplay. `world/` contains procedural generation, chunks, streaming, meshing, sunlight, and raycasting. `components/` defines entity data; `items/` defines blocks, rocks, and materials; `utils/` maps enums to localized labels.

## Making Changes

Implement screen behavior through `IGameState` and the existing `Game::changeState` mechanism. Keep reusable widgets in `../ui/`. Coordinate item or material schema changes with JSON data in `../../assets/data/` and resource loading.

World generation and chunk processing run asynchronously. Review cancellation, synchronization, captured object lifetimes, and result ownership before changing worker tasks. Keep GPU submission and resource disposal coordinated with the renderer. For voxel changes, check coordinate conversions, chunk dimensions in `world/consts.hpp`, neighbor boundaries, mesh visibility, and sunlight propagation together.

### X wrapping

The world wraps on X (the world map is generated tileable): tile `x` and `x + W` are the same column, where `W = world.width * 128`. Z ends at the map edges. Use `WorldWrap` (`world/world_wrap.hpp`, from `World::wrap()` or `ChunkManager::wrap()`). Never clamp or skip X at `0`/`W`.

- Stored coordinates (`ChunkCoord`, regions, edit keys, saved camera) are canonical, in `[0, W)`. Wrap X before any lookup or neighbor step (`wrapTile`/`wrapChunk`/`wrapRegion`). Map-less fixtures (`width == 0`) disable wrapping.
- Distances and draws use the copy nearest the camera (`deltaChunk`, `imageX`). Chunks are drawn at `camCX + deltaChunk(coord.x, camCX)`, and `LightVolumeManager::bind` gets that draw origin, so `block.vert` light lookups match. LOD patches stay canonical and are drawn shifted by 0 or ±W through `u_lod_shift` and `u_lod_patch`.
- Terrain noise must repeat every `W` tiles: pass `TerrainNeighborhood::periodX()` to `SoilGenerator::field`/`sample` and to `gradientField`. A lattice that cannot be periodic (the rotated soil-choice field) blends across the seam.
- Distant terrain wraps only when `TerrainLod::wrapsExactly(W, viewDistance)` holds: the width is a multiple of 16 regions (one LOD root), and the view distance plus two roots is at most half of `W`. New worlds are 512 regions wide. Other widths keep distant terrain bounded on X; their voxels still wrap.

### Terrain detail

`TerrainLod::Quality::chunkRadius` is 4, 6 or 8 on Low, Medium or High.
Gameplay applies the preset on world load. The same radius drives chunk
requests, draw bounds and structure handoff. `DetailCoverage` admits columns
two chunks inside the draw radius and keeps uploaded columns through the
next ring. The outer ring supplies mesh neighbors.

### Lighting

Tiles carry two 4-bit light levels: `sun_exposure` (`SunLightPropagator`) and `artificial_light` (`BlockLightPropagator`). `LightVolumeManager` encodes both into RGBA8 volumes (R sun, G block light, BA block-light chroma) sampled by `shaders/block/block.frag`. Light changes go through `ChunkManager::markLightChanged` and never remesh.

- Emission is data: an optional `"light"` object on a block (`items/light_emission.hpp`: `enabled`, `radius` up to 8192, `falloff` exponent of `1 - d/radius`, `intensity`, `color`, `cone`). Items and entities must reuse `LightEmission`; a placed or carried emitter is a `LightSource` (`world/light_sources.hpp`) with a world position and a free direction.
- Near field: within `LightEmission::NearRange` (15) blocks, `BlockLightPropagator` flood-fills each emitter on workers from immutable per-chunk opacity snapshots. The effective distance is the straight line plus the path's detour and translucency excess, so open space is round and walls cast soft shadows. Contributions add up (capped at 15), like the per-pixel far term, so the two hand over without a seam. Spotlights leave their tile only through front-hemisphere faces. A chunk depends only on emitters in its 3x3x3 neighborhood. Colored light stores chroma in a side buffer only for chunks a colored light reaches.
- `LightSourceRegistry` indexes every emissive tile (and, later, entity lights) in 128-tile buckets, with `openFaces`: a light whose usable faces are all opaque is `sealed()` and emits nothing beyond its tile (no far term, glow, glare or shadow slot).
- Far field: `LightRenderer` sends far lights (radius above 15, within the view distance) with ready shadow maps to `u_lights` for `block`, `terrain_lod` and `foliage`, evaluated per pixel. `LightShadows` gives the most important ones cube shadow maps (budget by terrain detail: 4/8/16 lights, 3/6/12 faces rendered per frame) in one R32F atlas (`world/light_shadow_math.hpp` and `shaders/shadow_sampling.sh` share the face and tile conventions). Faces re-render when a light is assigned or moves, when `ChunkManager::meshUploaded`/`removed` reports geometry in its radius, and (at most once per second) when `TerrainLodManager::drawnVersion()` changes. Casters are the detailed chunks drawn this frame plus `TerrainLodManager::submitShadowCasters`. Pending and over-budget lights keep their voxel near field. The shader checks the emitter’s open faces before sampling its shadow map, so a ray cannot leave the lamp through an opaque neighbor. The caster pass skips the light's own tile, sized to include `ChunkMesher::SeamOverlap` (change both together); `shadow_sampling.sh` switches to a conservative 4x4 test where a caster hugs the light, closing the one-texel cracks that walls built right around a lamp leave at 45 degrees.
- Sky light under structures beyond the voxel range: `SkyOcclusionMap` (`world/sky_occlusion_map.hpp`, published by `SkyHeightMap` from terrain, structure sky tops, edits and overrides) approximates voxel sunlight (`15 - distance to open sky`, by height) for `terrain_lod` and block-program draws without a light volume; `LightRenderer::setSkyOcclusion` uploads it. See `docs/handoff/structures.md`, step 2. Every program including `world_surface.sh` declares `u_sky_occlusion` (block stage 6, terrain_lod stage 7).
- Small lights beyond the voxel range: `LodLightMap` splats them into a 1024² (4 tiles per texel) glow texture around the camera, read by `terrain_lod` and by block-program joins and structures. Glare sprites for every unsealed light are one instanced draw in `DrawLayer::GAME_GLOW` (`RenderViews::Glow`); a shadowed light's glare hides when its cube shows an occluder right next to it.
- Every program that includes `shaders/world_surface.sh` must bind `LightRenderer::appendUniforms(list, Program)`, which picks the program's sampler stages; `TerrainLodManager::setExtraUniforms` takes the terrain_lod and block lists. Shadow passes use views from `RenderViews::ShadowBase`; `Renderer::beginFrame` orders `RenderViews::MaxShadowViews` of them before every other view each frame. Never let a view that samples the shadow atlas run before the passes that render it in the same frame: on RADV that crashes in `vkCmdBeginRenderPass`.
- Instanced foliage (plants, tree crowns) cannot bind per-chunk light volumes: the CPU samples sky through `SkyHeightMap` and lamp level and chroma from nearby tiles (`world/foliage_light.hpp`, chroma from `BlockLightPropagator::chroma`) and packs them into `i_data3.y`, which `foliage.vert` decodes. Renderers get the propagator through `setBlockLight`; without it near-field light is white.
- `u_light_info.y` darkens sky light (the debug SKY slider); it is not saved.
- Sky light is moving to the sky heightmap (`SkyHeightMap`, `world/sky_height_map.hpp`: terrain, trees and other structures through `structure_voxels::Source::skyTops`, `TerrainEdits`, and saved overrides) as its source of truth, so the ground no longer waits for the slabs above it: see `docs/handoff/sky-light.md`. Sky light is overhead only; sun direction is for rendering (shadows, normal maps). `SunLightPropagator` seeds direct light from it (a slab starts from the heightmap unless the slab above is loaded), so it never requests sky slabs, and spreads without waiting for neighbours being generated (a missing neighbour's halo comes from the heightmap; halos are assembled on workers from shared per-chunk opacity snapshots). Without a map (headless fixtures) it keeps the old full-column sweep and waiting. Every opacity change to a Ready chunk must reach `SunLightPropagator::markColumnDirty` (`WorldEditor` does), or the snapshots go stale. Gameplay "direct sunlight" is `SkyHeightMap::directSky` (0-15, attenuated by translucent tiles), never `Tile::lightLevel`. A new structure kind must answer `skyTops` exactly and cheaply.
- Gameplay light queries (how much and what colour of light reaches a position, loaded or not) are planned after the day/night cycle. Their sky input is `SkyHeightMap::skyAt`: see `docs/handoff/light-query.md`. Tiles hold only the voxel near field; do not build gameplay on `artificial_light` alone.
- Scaling to many far lights (e.g. ~100 lighthouses across a large map): current limits and planned fixes are in `docs/handoff/far-light-scaling.md`.

### Flora

Ground cover (grass) and plants follow `docs/handoff/flora.md` (its Progress section lists what exists). Neither is a block: a solid tile above the generated surface counts as a player edit and a distant structure voxel. Ground cover is per-tile data (`Tile::cover`, `coverState`) on the soil tile it grows on; plants will be per-chunk instances.

- Cover never changes `isSolid`, opacity, sunlight or `ChunkManager::isEdited`. Generation puts it on the surface topsoil tile (`GroundCoverGenerator`, `TerrainSampler::soil`); `WorldEditor` clears it on changed tiles and under placed blocks.
- Covered faces carry the overlay in `BlockVertex` `u`/`v`/`tint` (see `ChunkMesher::build`); any other producer of `BlockVertex` for the block program must write `u = -1`. Density comes from the `u_cover_volume` page beside each light volume page; every block-program draw must bind a sampler at `LightVolumeManager::CoverStage`.
- Distant terrain draws cover from `TerrainLod::Mesh::covers` (`u_cover_map`, stage 6) built by `SurfaceSampler::cover`; stepped LOD wall vertices store their column top in `u`. Keep `ground_cover_climate::at` the single climate source for tints so the detail/LOD hand-over matches.
- Ground cover ids are saved raw in tiles: keep new `ground_covers` files after existing ones.
- Plants are `FloraInstance`s in a per-chunk `FloraLayer` (`ChunkManager::flora`/`setFlora`, signal `floraChanged`), produced by the chunk job with `generateFlora` (`world/flora_generator.hpp`). Generation must stay a pure function of world coordinates and the seed: neighbouring chunks decide border plants independently (large plants by the local outranking rule within `FloraSpeciesTable::reach()`), and X-periodic hashing keeps the seam invisible. Layers are immutable: replace them, never mutate a shared one. Flora ids are saved raw: keep new `flora` files after existing ones.
- `FloraRenderer` draws plants instanced (`shaders/foliage/`), meshes from `FloraMeshBuilder`; see `docs/handoff/flora.md` section 9 for limits and the smoke-test switches.
- Game time is `WorldClock` (`world/world_clock.hpp`, saved as `worldDays` in `world.json`). Plant stages are analytic in the clock's day; `FloraSimulation` advances layers lazily (on load and in a slow sweep): dead plants are removed, populations continue only through seeds (dropped once per seeding cycle, germinating against local crowding toward the generated density; deterministic and deduplicated), chunks unloaded for a year or more are re-sampled at equilibrium (`regenerateFlora`, epoch-salted placement with fixed cluster patches), harvests regrow, and cover regrows on exposed topsoil. Never tick plants per frame or per day across the world. Large plants need level ground under their footprint and nothing rising beside it, and `FloraMeshBuilder` keeps their canopy within the footprint plus 0.3 m. Rare species are data (tiny `density_per_100m2`; `persistent_sites` keeps their sites across epochs by placing them with the unsalted seed, `FloraGenerationInput::clusterSeed`).
- Modder-facing documentation of the data formats is `docs/modding/plants.md`: update it with any change to ground cover, foliage material or flora schemas.

### Trees

Trees follow `docs/handoff/trees.md` (its Progress section lists what exists); modder docs are `docs/modding/trees.md` (update with any `Tree` schema change).

- `Tree` (`items/tree.hpp`, `assets/data/trees/`) shares the flora placement parser (`flora_json::placement`) and foliage materials. Tree ids are saved raw: keep new `trees` files after existing ones.
- Trunks and limbs at least `TreeVoxelRadius` thick are living wood: blocks with `natural_growth` (`naturalGrowthBlocks` gives the id set). Core and bark share ownership and support rules. Edit detection treats crown wood as air and root wood as its displaced substrate. Player-placeable logs and planks use other block ids.
- Ordinary trees reach at most `TreeMaxReach` from their anchor (generation reads terrain only within one region of a chunk); massive trees are landmarks placed from a world-wide list (`placeTreeLandmarks`).
- Each tree is placed and built (`generateRegionTrees`, skeleton included) from the inputs of the region holding its anchor. Chunks wait for those region sets through `ChunkManager::prepareTrees`/`treeColumn`, so neighbours agree on skeletons. `tree_wood_lod::columnVoxels` adapts ordinary and landmark wood to the shared structure voxel column.
- Landmark wood is drawn at distance as structure voxels: `TreeRenderer::landmarkVoxels` turns each landmark into a `structure_voxels::Source` (`tree_wood_lod::voxels`) for `TerrainLodManager::setStructures` (`StructureLod`), and those landmarks' crowns skip bark prisms (`Detail::DistantCrown`). Any generated structure is drawn this way: implement `Source::rasterize(level, slab)` (see `docs/handoff/structures.md`); `StructureLod` plans full tiles or 2x/4x cells per slab by distance, rebuilds only the slabs whose level or revision changes, draws coarse slabs merged per group of 4³, and supplies distant sky shade through the source's `skyTops` projection. Report `occupancy()` and `changedSince()` too: both bound the work.
- Far forests are billboards: `regionBillboards` (placement only, same positions as full generation) built by low-priority jobs from `ChunkManager::wantBillboards`, drawn per species with a fade-in (`i_data3.w`) that complements the distant meshes' fade-out.
- Crowns (foliage, branches thinner than a block) are drawn by `TreeRenderer` with the foliage program in tree mode (`u_foliage.z = 1`). Ordinary trees share skeletons per species variant and quarter turn (`TreeSpeciesTable::variant`), so their crown meshes are shared and instanced; keep `TreeSkeletonInput::rotation` and the foliage shader's yaw convention identical.
- During play `TreeSimulation` grows wood in loaded chunks (through `WorldEditor`), turns wood removals that sever a branch or trunk into cuts, and plants trees (debug tool). What a cut trunk does is data (`cut.fell`): it falls (today the part above disappears; later a physics hazard) or settles onto the stump (massive trees: a copy of the pruned part one tile lower, `TreeSegment::appear` at the cut). Wood a cut changes is rasterized per chunk on the pool and applied over the following updates. Cuts and planted trees live in `TreeOverrideMap` (`ChunkManager::treeOverrides`, saved in `structures.dat`); change them only with `setTreeOverrides` plus `updateTree`, so tree sets, columns and in-flight jobs follow. Skeletons with cuts must come from `applyTreeCuts` in record order.
- Massive trees span hundreds of thousands of chunks: never rasterize or allocate a whole tree's wood box at once. Work per chunk or slab (`TreeSimulation` clearing queue, `structure_voxels`), keep crown meshes on the pool (`TreeRenderer`), and check costs with `tree_benchmark` (`docs/handoff/trees.md`, "Massive tree performance").
- Massive species are immortal. Shallow subsurface roots use a separate skeleton and bounds so their shape never renumbers saved crown cuts. Root replacement only occurs in natural soil or rock. Chunk metadata tracks displaced substrate, ownership and player terrain edits. v3 slabs restore prototype root substrate and regrow the shallow shape. v1 and v2 slabs lack edit provenance and retain their original terrain.
- Chunk jobs stamp level-zero structure voxels only into air and share their exact `Source::skyTops` projections with the sky heightmap. Register other generated kinds through `ChunkManager::setGeneratedStructureKinds` with their horizontal reach. `placeTreeWood` uses the same stamp for legacy tree backfill. The chunk's `TreeStamp` (day and `TreeVersion`) is saved in the FLOR blob header. Bump `TreeVersion` for changes to existing crown skeletons, rasterization or placement. The root metadata has its own migration through the chunk codec.

## Persistence

Each world is a folder `saves/<seed>_<YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS>/` (see `world/save/save_catalog.hpp`):

- `world.json`: `WorldMeta` (format/generator versions, master seed, timestamps, camera).
- `world_map.dat`: `world_map_codec` sections `GENS`, `RGNS`, `RIVR`. Preview meshes are not stored.
- `regions/r.<x>.<z>.dat`: `region_file` sections `CHNK` (one `chunk_codec` blob per chunk, local index `slabY*64 + cz*8 + cx`; v5 records reservoir eligibility, v4 stores the shallow root shape and optional substrate and edit metadata, v1 through v4 still decode), `TOPS` (column ceilings) and `FLOR` (one `flora_codec` blob of plants per saved chunk, same index; a saved chunk without one regrows its generated plants; v3 blobs also carry the chunk's `TreeStamp`, and a chunk without one gets its trees once).
- `terrain_edits.dat`: `terrain_edits_codec` section `EDIT` v3, the world-wide index of edited chunk columns that distant terrain shows (`world/terrain_edits.hpp`). It includes generated structure suppression masks and caps for edited reservoir columns. The v1 reader loads additions without masks. The v2 reader restores fixed water summaries. Region files hold the authoritative tiles.
- `water.dat`: authoritative `RSVR` v5 section with standing volume, local cell credits, river holds, the budget clock, certified surface breaches and dams, sealed generated springs, detached lake compartments, and dug channel paths and arrivals. Load it before requesting chunks. The chunk codec omits projected water. See [water](../../docs/handoff/water.md#5-add-local-finite-volume-flow-and-edited-connections).
- `light_sources.dat`: `light_sources_codec` section `LITE` (v2: position, block id, open faces), the world-wide index of emissive tiles (see Lighting). Also derived.
- `sky.dat`: `sky_codec` section `SKYO` v1, sky heightmap overrides (`SkyHeightMap`, `docs/handoff/sky-light.md`): tile columns whose loaded tiles differ from terrain, trees and `terrain_edits.dat` (holes dug into tree wood). The rest of the heightmap is rebuilt on load. `GameplayState` writes it with `terrain_edits.dat`.
- `structures.dat`: authoritative `TREE` v4 and `VOXL` v1 sections (tree overrides and per-kind voxel overrides). The save catalog imports `trees.dat` when the new file is absent. Region `STRB` records the structure baseline for each saved slab so chunk loading can reconcile a new revision with the saved tiles.

Every sub-seed derives from `WorldGenSettings::seed` through `WorldGenSettings::fromSeed`. Bump `WorldMeta::GeneratorVersion` when chunk generation output changes.

`LocationManager` owns region residency. With a `RegionStore` it opens a region asynchronously and registers its chunks with their saved blobs; `ChunkManager` decodes those chunks instead of generating them. Unloading snapshots chunks whose `persistDirty` flag is set and queues a write. In play, regions unload gradually (`onPlayerRegionChanged` -> `update()`, whole chunk columns within `LocationManager::UnloadBudget` per frame, each snapshotted as it goes); a region loaded again meanwhile, and `saveAll`, finish it first. Per-chunk `removed` listeners must stay cheap: defer per-column work to the next update (as `SunLightPropagator` does). `RegionStore` runs at most one job per region at a time and keeps recently released regions warm in RAM. Callers of `ChunkManager::request` never see where data came from.

- Distant terrain shows edits through `TerrainEdits`. Each edited column stores its ground and water, additions and replacements above the generated surface, and suppressed generated structure voxels. `TerrainEditTracker` recomputes columns from `WorldEditor::blockChanged` and from saved chunks that differ from generation (`ChunkManager::isEdited`, enabled when the save's `generatorVersion` is at least `WorldMeta::SurfaceVersion`, the oldest generator with today's surface heights). Bump `SurfaceVersion` only when surface heights change. Edits that bypass `WorldEditor` appear in the index after their chunk reloads. `GameplayState` writes `terrain_edits.dat` during autosave and on exit.
- Lighting is derived and never saved. `SunLightPropagator` and `BlockLightPropagator` writes must not set `persistDirty`. The one exception is `light_sources.dat` (`light_sources_codec`, section `LITE`): a derived world-wide index of emissive tiles (position, block id, open faces), so lighthouses and towns glow while their regions are unloaded. Emission values are re-read from block definitions on load, and chunk loads reconcile the index. `GameplayState` writes it with `terrain_edits.dat`.
- Block ids are assigned in data-file order and saved raw, so new block files must sort after existing ones (e.g. `blocks/test_light.json` after `blocks/soils.json`) or existing saves remap.
- Gameplay edits to tiles must go through `withChunkLocked` followed by `ChunkManager::markModified`, so the chunk is saved and remeshed. Prefer `WorldEditor` (`world/world_editor.hpp`) for block placement and removal: it also remeshes border neighbors, raises column tops and recomputes sunlight.
- `GameplayState` autosaves one region per frame every 300 s. Its destructor saves every region, flushes the store and writes `world.json`. Keep `regionStore` declared before the chunk systems that use it.
- New persistent data (units, factions, history) goes in a new fourcc section in the owning file. Use `world_map.dat` or a new root file for world-scope data, and region files for region-scope data. Older readers keep unknown sections untouched and write them back. Add round-trip and malformed-input tests under `tests/unit/game/world/save_codec_tests.cpp` or `tests/integration/region_persistence_tests.cpp`.

## Build & Validation

From the repository root, run `cmake --build build --target app --parallel`. Create a world, enter gameplay, move across chunk boundaries, and return through the affected states. For persistence changes, also travel 3+ regions away and back, leave with Escape, continue the world from LOAD WORLD, and check `saves/` for leftover `.tmp` or `.corrupt` files. For generation changes, record seed and settings so failures can be reproduced. Check cancellation and exit during background work, and inspect lighting and mesh seams for voxel changes.
